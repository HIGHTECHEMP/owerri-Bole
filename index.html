Taste of Owerri

OWERI-BOLE brings the authentic street food culture of Owerri, Imo State to your table. Our signature Bole (fire-roasted plantain) is prepared using traditional charcoal methods passed down through generations.

From the famous Ofe Owerri - the "King of Soups" made with ugu, okazi, and cocoyam thickener - to Nkwobi (spicy cow foot delicacy), every dish tells a story of Igbo culinary excellence.
